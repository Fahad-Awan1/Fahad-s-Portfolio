01Overview

ProjectOverview

Pehnawa is a clothing brand selling menswear, womenswear and kids' clothing. The dashboard opens on the numbers that matter to the owner: total revenue, this month's sales, returns and profit, sales split by men, women and kids, monthly expenses against budget, the sales flow against target, stock capacity and recent orders.

From the sidebar, staff move into analytics, orders, returns and exchanges, stock levels with low and out-of-stock alerts, suppliers and purchase orders, and the customer list. Notifications flag new orders and items that are running low, so nothing sells out unnoticed.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Store dashboardRevenue, monthly sales, returns, profit and the split across men, women and kids.
  2. 02Sales analyticsPerformance trends, sales by category and top products.
  3. 03Orders, returns and exchangesEvery order with its payment and status, plus returns and exchanges.
  4. 04Stock levelsShelf fill, low-stock and out-of-stock alerts for every product.
  5. 05Suppliers and purchasingSupplier records and purchase orders to restock quickly.
  6. 06Customers and notificationsCustomer history, plus alerts for new orders and low stock.
