01Overview

ProjectOverview

HOCL Labs is a skincare brand built around hypochlorous acid, with the tagline "Powerful. Pure. Gentle." The homepage opens on a full-screen cloud scene with floating bubbles and the Axirya Face Mist front and centre, then moves into a product showcase, results and articles so visitors can learn about the formula and shop in the same visit.

The site has a full shop with filters for mists and tonic pads, dedicated product pages, an articles library sorted by topic, an about page and a contact form. A search overlay and a slide-out cart are available from the header on every page.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Cloud-scene heroA full-screen sky with floating bubbles, a layered headline and the hero product, set up for a strong first impression.
  2. 02Shop with filtersAll products in one grid, filterable by Mists and Tonic Pads, with prices shown up front.
  3. 03Product pagesEach product has its own page with a clear description and a Shop Now action.
  4. 04Cart and searchA slide-out cart with a running total, plus a full-screen search across products and articles.
  5. 05Articles librarySkin science, routines, product education, real stories and expert insights, filterable by category.
  6. 06Contact formA short enquiry form with interest options and a clear consent line.
