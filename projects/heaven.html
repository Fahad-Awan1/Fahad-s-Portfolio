01Overview

ProjectOverview

Heaven is a boutique estate agency selling and renting luxury homes across Los Angeles. The site needed to sell a feeling as much as a listing, so it opens on a warm, golden-hour hero with an elegant serif headline, a featured home card and a time-aware greeting that invites visitors to take a 60-second matching quiz.

A property search sits right below, followed by featured homes, a scroll-driven Three.js story that builds a house piece by piece as you read the agency's four-step approach, and a neighbourhood explorer with walkability, schools and nature scores. Separate pages cover every listing, buying, renting, selling with an instant valuation, the agents and a journal, with favourites, a compare tool and the Halo assistant available throughout.
