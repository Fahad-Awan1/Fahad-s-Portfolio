01Overview

ProjectOverview

Oiyo is a garden café that sells calm as much as coffee, so the site slows the visitor down: soft colours, a classic serif, smooth Lenis scrolling and a hero that tells you whether the café is open right now.

The centrepiece is the Ritual Builder. Visitors pick a base, a milk and a sweetener, watch the cup change as they go, see the price update, and add the drink to a tray. Around it sit the seasonal signature picks, the café's story, a journal of seasonal notes and a reservation call-to-action, plus a small chat assistant called Dew that answers questions about hours, the menu and booking.
