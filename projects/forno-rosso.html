01Overview

ProjectOverview

Forno Rosso makes Neapolitan pizza: slow-rising dough, a wood-fired oven at 450°C and simple Italian ingredients. The site needed to be as warm and lively as the pizzeria itself.

The homepage opens on a rotating carousel of pizzas over a deep-red oven arch, with curved type that moves as you scroll. GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis drive the scroll story, and Three.js adds WebGL depth. A full-screen menu links to the Menu, Our Kitchen, Experiences, About and Contact pages, with an Order button always in reach.
