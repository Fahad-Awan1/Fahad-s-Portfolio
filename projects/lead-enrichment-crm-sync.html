01Overview

ProjectOverview

Sales teams lose hours researching leads and typing them into the CRM, and the data still ends up patchy. This workflow does that work the moment a lead arrives.

Each lead is enriched with company and contact information, checked against existing records to avoid duplicates, and summarised and scored by an AI model. The clean record is synced to the CRM and the right person is notified, ready to follow up.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Automatic enrichmentCompany and contact details added to every new lead.
  2. 02AI summary and scoreA short summary and priority score, so the team knows who to call first.
  3. 03Duplicate checksExisting contacts are matched and updated instead of duplicated.
  4. 04CRM auto-syncClean records written to the CRM with no manual entry.
  5. 05Team notificationsThe right person is alerted as soon as a qualified lead lands.
  6. 06Runs on every leadTriggered automatically from forms, ads and other sources.
