01Overview

ProjectOverview

Medora gives hospital management one clear view of what is happening across departments. The main dashboard shows patient admissions by department, where patients come from, bed occupancy and today's revenue, and every chart is built from scratch with SVG and CSS, without a chart library.

Separate screens cover analytics (length of stay, readmissions, admissions against discharges, gender and age breakdowns, top diagnoses), billing (monthly revenue in PKR, insurance claims, invoices and outstanding dues), the pharmacy (stock levels, dispensing activity and expiry alerts) and a calendar with doctors on duty and a weekly schedule. Any screen can be exported to CSV or printed.
