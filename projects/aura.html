01Overview

ProjectOverview

Aura is a fashion storefront that needed to feel like a luxury magazine without losing the basics of a good shop. The homepage opens on an oversized serif STYLE headline with a model cut out in front of it, then moves straight into shopping: categories for women, men, accessories and footwear, and a New Arrivals rail with prices and sale prices.

Every product can be added to the bag in one tap, and the bag, wishlist and search are always one click away in the header. Cart state is handled in Zustand so the bag stays in sync across pages, and GSAP and React Three Fiber drive the motion and 3D touches.
