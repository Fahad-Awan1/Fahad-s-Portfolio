01Overview

ProjectOverview

Missed calls are missed customers, but staffing a phone line around the clock is expensive. This agent picks up every call, talks with the caller in natural language and works through a structured conversation built in LangGraph.

It answers common questions, qualifies the caller against the client's criteria and books an appointment or routes the call to a person when needed. Every call ends with a summary and the key details pushed to the team, so follow-up starts with full context.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Natural phone conversationsVoice AI that listens, understands and replies in real time.
  2. 02Structured call flowsLangGraph keeps the conversation on track while still handling detours.
  3. 03Lead qualificationAsks the client's qualifying questions and records the answers.
  4. 04Appointment bookingBooks a slot during the call when the caller is ready.
  5. 05Call summariesA short summary and key details sent to the team after every call.
  6. 06Human handoffTransfers to a person when a call needs one.
