01Overview

ProjectOverview

Ember & Olive is a restaurant ERP built for a busy Lahore restaurant. The overview screen shows the day at a glance: live revenue against the sales target, orders and guests served, daily sales analytics, kitchen load by station, live kitchen tickets, recent orders and staff on shift, with an AI question bar for quick answers.

Every other part of the restaurant has its own screen: an order board that moves tickets from New to Cooking, Ready, Served and Paid, a POS for new orders with receipts, the menu with prices, margins and availability, a floor plan with table status and reservations, staff clock-in and weekly schedules, stock against par levels with purchase orders, and revenue reports with CSV export.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Live overviewRevenue against target, orders, guests, kitchen load and live tickets on one screen.
  2. 02Kitchen order boardTickets move from New to Cooking, Ready, Served and Paid as the kitchen works.
  3. 03POS and receiptsTake new dine-in, takeaway or delivery orders and print receipts.
  4. 04Tables and reservationsA floor plan by zone with seating, open bills and upcoming bookings.
  5. 05Inventory and purchasingStock against par levels, low-stock alerts, purchase orders and deliveries.
  6. 06Reports and exportRevenue charts, channels, top dishes and payment methods, exportable to CSV.
