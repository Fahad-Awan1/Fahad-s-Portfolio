01Overview

ProjectOverview

Savoria runs several branches, so the overview starts with the group's total revenue and each branch's progress against its monthly target, followed by today's sales, food cost, orders and average ticket, profit against food cost over time, the ingredient budget and recent orders.

Dedicated screens cover orders (a kitchen board, orders by channel and peak hours), the menu (dishes, top sellers and revenue by category), reservations (a floor plan, tonight's guests and bookings by hour), staff (the team, shift coverage and top performers), inventory (stock levels and reordering) and reports (revenue trends, branch performance and sales by category, with export).
