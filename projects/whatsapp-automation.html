01Overview

ProjectOverview

Customers expect a quick answer on WhatsApp, often outside working hours. This n8n workflow connects the client's WhatsApp Business number to an AI model and their existing tools.

New messages get an instant, on-brand reply. Common questions are answered automatically, lead details are collected and saved, and scheduled follow-ups go out without anyone remembering to send them. Conversations that need a human are flagged and passed to the team.

02Key features

KeyFeatures

  1. 01Instant repliesEvery new message is acknowledged within seconds, day or night.
  2. 02AI answersCommon questions answered in natural language using the client's information.
  3. 03Lead captureNames, needs and contact details collected and saved automatically.
  4. 04Automated follow-upsScheduled reminders and updates sent without manual work.
  5. 05Team handoffConversations that need a person are flagged and routed.
  6. 06Built in n8nA visual workflow the client's team can see and adjust.
