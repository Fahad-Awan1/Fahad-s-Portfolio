01Overview

ProjectOverview

Drivia rents premium cars, from Teslas to V10 supercars, in Dubai, London, Milan, Nice, Miami and Los Angeles. The site had to feel as polished as the cars while making booking effortless. The hero pairs a cinematic car shot with a clear promise and a “Book in 2 minutes” route, and a search bar lets visitors pick a city, dates and car type straight away.

Below, a drag-to-spin fleet showcase lets visitors browse cars with specs and day rates, a trip-mood picker suggests cars for a coastal cruise or a business trip, and a four-step explainer takes the anxiety out of renting. Dedicated pages cover the full fleet, each car, pricing, the six locations on an interactive Leaflet map, and a multi-step booking flow, with a chat assistant on hand throughout.
